# Goals

### G1 · Drop in a song and be playing it within seconds, with no setup. — MUST
**Why:** Bringing your own music is the hook. Any friction before the first target kills it as a warm-up.
**Acceptance checks:**
- [ ] Uploading an MP3, WAV or OGG file (plus M4A/AAC on Chromium) starts analysis immediately; an unsupported or broken file shows a plain-words error instead of failing silently.
- [ ] A 4-minute song is analysed in under 5 s on a mid-range laptop, and the page never freezes for more than 100 ms while it runs.
- [ ] Uploaded songs are stored in the browser (IndexedDB) and are still in the song list after a reload, with no re-upload.
- [ ] The preset picker shown when adding a song has sensible defaults, so upload → playing takes at most one extra click.
- [ ] The same song, preset and level always produce the same chart (seeded generator), so scores are comparable between runs.
- [ ] Any song gets a chart with no hand-authoring: generation uses only the song's analysis, the preset and the level (D7).
**Implemented by:** T1, T5, T6, T8, T9, T17

### G2 · Targets land on the beat of the music. — MUST
**Why:** If timing feels off, it's not a rhythm drill, and an automatic chart has no fix-up step (D3).
**Acceptance checks:**
- [ ] On generated test songs (drum loops at 70–190 BPM, with swing, noise and a quiet intro), detected tempo is within 1% and beat times within 20 ms of the truth (vitest, node).
- [ ] During play, every target's spawn and hit time is computed from the AudioContext clock; drift between chart and audio stays under 10 ms across a 4-minute song.
- [ ] Pausing stops music and targets together, and resuming continues in sync.
- [ ] An audio offset (from a short tap-along calibration or set by hand) shifts every target; after calibration, the player's taps average within ±20 ms of the beat.
**Implemented by:** T2, T3, T10, T11, T19

### G3 · Six target types each spawn on the beat, behave in time with the music and vanish if missed. — MUST
**Why:** A mix of target types is the core idea (D1, D2): each one trains a different aim skill inside one musical run.
**Acceptance checks:**
- [ ] Flick shapes, strafers, holds, bursts, peekers and decoys all appear in charts.
- [ ] Every target spawns on a beat (or eighth beat for bursts) and is removed when its hit window ends without a hit.
- [ ] Strafers change direction on beats; holds move along their path in step with the beat; peekers show and hide on beats.
- [ ] Hitting a decoy counts as a mistake and breaks the combo.
**Implemented by:** T1, T4, T6, T13, T14, T15

### G4 · It is a first-class Azimuth drill: levels, stars, debrief, Logbook and coaching. — MUST
**Why:** Players already understand Azimuth's progression. A rhythm drill outside it would feel like a toy.
**Acceptance checks:**
- [ ] Registered in DRILL_DEFS with 10 levels and 1/2/3★ thresholds, and listed in Training like the other drills; not added to DAILY and the streak is unchanged (D15).
- [ ] Picking a difficulty plays the highest unlocked level inside its band; a star on a level unlocks the next level in that band (D10, D17).
- [ ] Its DrillReport gives a 0–100 score, the difficulty and level played, timing stats (mean early/late offset in ms) and hit rate per target type.
- [ ] Flick-type hits produce FlickRecords, so the existing coach findings and film room work on them.
- [ ] Runs appear in the Logbook's recent sessions.
**Implemented by:** T5, T7, T13, T18

### G5 · Fun first: it feels like a rhythm game and a warm-up you want to play, not a test: flowing, musical, creative and satisfying, while it trains every aim skill along the way. — MUST
**Why:** The brief: a warm-up you want to start, Beat Saber and Guitar Hero as references. The human: fun is the aim; this is the step away from Azimuth's rigid drills (D19).
**Acceptance checks:**
- [ ] Every hit gives instant feedback (sound, flash and a judgement word: Perfect, Early or Late) within one frame of the click; a missed target shows Miss as it vanishes.
- [ ] A hit within the Perfect width of the target's beat scores more and adds more to the combo than a hit elsewhere in the window (D4).
- [ ] A combo counter and multiplier build on consecutive hits and visibly break on a miss or a decoy hit.
- [ ] The synthwave scene reacts to the music: the grid scrolls at the tempo and glows on the beat (D6).
- [ ] Playtest: the human plays it on their own songs and says it feels on-beat and fun enough to warm up with.
**Implemented by:** T7, T12, T16, T19
