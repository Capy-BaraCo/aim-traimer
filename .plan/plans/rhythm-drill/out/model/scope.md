# Scope

**Build:**
- Song upload and an in-browser song library (IndexedDB): add, list, delete, with an empty state that asks for a song
- Fully automatic tempo, beat, section and loudness analysis as pure, tested functions
- Data-driven preset format and a seeded chart generator (D7)
- One style preset, Mixed (D8), plus a preset and difficulty picker when adding a song (D9, D10)
- Six target types: flick shapes, strafers, holds, bursts, peekers, decoys
- Music playback on the AudioContext clock with an audio offset and a tap-along calibration
- Synthwave scene (D6) with the four beat cues: fly-in, closing ring, pulse and beat strip (D5)
- Window + beat-bonus scoring (D4), combo, hit feedback, and a debrief with timing and per-type stats
- Registration as a normal Azimuth drill in Training (levels, stars, Logbook, coach)
