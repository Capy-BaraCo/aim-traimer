# Risks

### R1 · Notes drift out of sync with the music. The game clock runs on requestAnimationFrame and freezes on pause, while music plays on the AudioContext clock, and output latency (especially Bluetooth headphones) adds 20–250 ms. — high likelihood / high impact
**Mitigation:** Position every target from song time (AudioContext time minus output latency minus the audio offset), never from accumulated frame time. Pausing stops the music source and freezes song time; it does not suspend the shared AudioContext, so menu sounds keep working. A tap-along calibration and a manual slider set the audio offset.
**Mitigated by:** T10, T11

### R2 · Automatic analysis picks the wrong tempo or beat phase on some songs (live recordings, swing, tempo changes, long quiet intros), so targets feel off-beat and there's no way for the player to fix it. — medium likelihood / high impact
**Mitigation:** Use onset-strength autocorrelation with a tempo prior (prefer 80–180 BPM, resolving half and double tempo) and track beats per section rather than one global grid. Score the analysis confidence; when it's low, place targets on detected onsets (audible hits) instead of a strict grid. Test against generated songs with swing, tempo ramps and noise in vitest.
**Mitigated by:** T3

### R3 · Four cues at once (fly-in, ring, pulse, beat strip) clutter dense charts at high levels, so players can't see the targets they're aiming at. — medium likelihood / medium impact
**Mitigation:** Keep each cue thin and low-contrast except at the moment it matters (ring and flash on the beat), cap targets on screen per level, and let the beat strip be switched off in Settings. Playtest level 10 on a fast song before tuning.
**Mitigated by:** T16, T19

### R4 · Section detection mislabels the song (e.g. calls a quiet verse a peak, or finds no peak in a flat electronic loop), so charts feel random or monotonous. — medium likelihood / medium impact
**Mitigation:** Classify sections by smoothed energy tiers and change points rather than trying to name verse or chorus; enforce a minimum section length (4 bars, snapped to bar boundaries); and fall back to energy-only density when the change points are weak. Unit-test on generated songs with known quiet/loud structure.
**Mitigated by:** T4, T19
