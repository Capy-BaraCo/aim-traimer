# Look and feel

The human's concept laid out like a Guitar Hero chart: two bars of 4/4 run left to right, with one row per target type. Flick targets of different shapes (circle, diamond, triangle) appear a beat early (dashed line) and must be hit on their beat; a missed one fades and is gone. A player figure (strafer) spawns on beat 3 and changes strafe direction on every beat until it leaves on beat 3 of the next bar. The third row is a placeholder for other target types still to choose.

Proposed choreography for combining all four cues on one target (D5). Time runs left to right in beats. Fly-in: the target launches from the distance one beat before its spawn beat and lands at its spot on the spawn beat. Ring: from landing, a ring closes onto it and touches it exactly on its target beat. Pulse: the target pulses gently with the music and flashes bright on its target beat. Strip: its icon scrolls along the beat strip at the bottom of the screen and crosses the hit line on the same beat. The hit window (light band) runs from landing to window end, with the Perfect band (gold) centred on the target beat.

The Mixed preset's starting matrix (D8), to be tuned in playtesting (T19). Rows are section kinds and columns are target types; each cell is the probability that a spawn in that kind of section is that type, and each row sums to 1. Intro: flick .50, strafer .20, hold .30. Calm: flick .30, strafer .20, hold .30, peeker .20. Build: flick .35, burst .20, strafer .15, peeker .20, decoy .10. Peak: flick .30, burst .25, strafer .20, hold .05, peeker .10, decoy .10. Outro: flick .30, strafer .30, hold .40. Empty cells are 0. Targets per bar before the level's density multiplier: intro 1, calm 2, build ramping from 2 to 4 across the section, peak 4, outro 1, where a burst counts as one target. Flick targets split into shapes circle .50, diamond .25, triangle .25. Intros lead with flicks and outros with holds; bursts and decoys appear only from the build onwards.
