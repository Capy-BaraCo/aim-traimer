# Glossary

Use these exact terms in code, UI copy and docs.
- **Overdrive**: The drill this plan builds (drill id 'overdrive').
- **Song**: An audio file the player added to their library. There is no built-in song (D11). Never call it: track (means tracking aim in Azimuth), music file.
- **Beat**: One pulse of the song's tempo (a quarter note in 4/4). Targets spawn and must be hit relative to beats.
- **Bar**: A group of beats, normally four. The generator works bar by bar. Never call it: measure.
- **Section**: A stretch of the song (at least 4 bars, endBar exclusive). Intro and outro are positional (the first and last sections only); middle sections are classified by energy as calm, build or peak. Never call it: part, verse/chorus.
- **Chart**: The timed list of targets (and walls) for one song at one preset and level. `Chart` in code. Never call it: beatmap, map.
- **Style preset**: Data that shapes a chart: target-type weights and targets per bar for each section kind, flick shape split, and optional multipliers on target parameters. `Preset` in code. MVP: Mixed only. Never call it: theme, mode.
- **Difficulty**: Easy, Normal, Hard or Expert, chosen per song. Maps to a band of drill levels (1–3, 4–6, 7–8, 9–10) that unlocks on its own (D17).
- **Level**: The drill's 1–10 step, as in every Azimuth drill. The chart is built at one level inside the chosen difficulty's band.
- **Target**: One thing to shoot that the chart spawns: a flick shape, strafer, hold, burst, peeker or decoy. `ChartTarget` in a chart. Never call it: note, block.
- **Target type**: A kind of target with its own look and behaviour (D2). `kind` on ChartTarget.
- **Target beat**: The beat a target should be hit on. `targetTime` in code (seconds of song time).
- **Hit window**: From landTime (the target lands) to windowEnd. Any hit inside counts; when it ends without a hit the target is missed and removed.
- **Judgement**: How a hit is graded: Perfect (within the Perfect width of the target beat), Early or Late (elsewhere in the window), or Miss. Never call it: Good, Great.
- **Song time**: Seconds into the song as the player hears it: AudioContext time since the music started, minus output latency (baseLatency + outputLatency), minus the audio offset. Everything in a run is timed from it.
- **Beat cues**: The four ways the beat is shown (D5): fly-in, closing ring, pulse and the beat strip.
- **Beat strip**: The Guitar Hero-style HUD strip at the bottom of the screen where icons of upcoming targets scroll to a hit line. Never call it: highway, lane.
- **Audio offset**: How late the player hears the audio, in ms. It is subtracted in song time, so a positive offset makes targets arrive later. Set by the tap-along calibration or by hand.
